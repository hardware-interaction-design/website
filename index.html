The Hardware Interaction Design community.

This is the first in-person event from Hardware Interaction Design, a community for the interaction designers, UX researchers, industrial designers, and engineers who design, research, prototype, and ship physical products — because designing a button, a dial, or a wearable comes with challenges that software communities rarely discuss.
